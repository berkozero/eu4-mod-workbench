# Asset provenance and redistribution

## EU4 material

Europa Universalis IV belongs to Paradox Interactive. This is an unofficial modding project. Full native mission files, GUI artwork, bitmap fonts and compiled HTML previews are **not included** in the public repository. The builder reads those from the user's installed game to create a local package and preview. Source references, identifiers and authored research explain how the extension integrates with the game. Do not commit the generated `dist/` directory.

## Custom images

The custom mission portraits and loading painting were generated for this project. Current game-sized assets live under `mods/teutonic/content/gfx/` and `mods/teutonic/assets/`. Their prompts are retained in that assets folder. Native mission icons are preserved by reference, not repainted. Older custom portraits remain for compatibility with the preserved legacy sprite definitions.

## Music

Preserve the full [music credits](mods/teutonic/content/MUSIC-CREDITS.txt), including source links and conversion/normalization details, with redistribution. `maintheme.ogg` is a second copy of the credited Gregorian Chant track.
