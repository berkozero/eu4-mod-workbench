This standalone HTML contains the configured missions, their artwork, requirements, rewards and relevant event outcomes. It reads no save and makes no changes to EU4.

The tree uses the installed game's five-column 104 × 152 grid, its 103 × 123 frames, 64px portraits, condition/reward markers and Garamond bitmap font. Slots, positions and arrows come from the source mod. Original coordinates are preserved. Arrows show local progression; earlier milestones in the checklist remain mandatory. Branch-exclusive missions can be shown together for inspection; check the assignment fixtures for each campaign phase.

Arrow routing approximates EU4. The game's declared title box is only 22px high; the preview shows up to two lines on the plaque and flags longer captions. Exact engine clipping and tooltips still need an authorized runtime check.

In the planning sandbox, mark real-world requirements as assumed, complete a mission, and read the event outcomes. The planner records completion marks and hypothetical choices only; resources, diplomacy and wars are not simulated. It does not simulate wars, resources, AI acceptance, modifier expiry or elapsed game time. A green check means your planning assumption, not a verified save condition.
