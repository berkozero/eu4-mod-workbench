# Crusader continuation: current extension

This is the current 56-mission extension. Eight optional support missions accompany the native campaign; 48 continue its northern, eastern, military, southern and institutional routes. Mission scripts and reward descriptions are authored in `../missions.json`; the generated HTML includes the 32 original entries too. All fictional continuations are identified below.

The tree preserves the 32 native coordinates, conditions, rewards and icons (with the requested branch visibility/completion gate). It has 79 drawable arrows and 21 explicit earlier-milestone requirements. These represent the same 100 prerequisite relationships. The old cut-off and broken-arrow defects are covered by the shared regression suite.

## C04 — A Learned Clergy

Slot 3, row 11. Required missions: C01, I08.

**Conditions:** Remain Catholic. Own 8 universities and 12 cathedrals, and employ a level-3-or-higher Theologian.

**Rewards:** Learned Clergy: -5% advisor cost, +1 advisor pool (25 years).

**Historical root:** Commanderies and central administration.

**Fictional continuation:** The intermediate schools now support an institutional continuation beyond the vanilla cathedral ending.

## C06 — The Order’s Arsenals

Slot 1, row 12. Required missions: V30, C02.

**Conditions:** Remain Catholic. Own 4 weapons manufactories, field 40 artillery regiments and reach military technology 18.

**Rewards:** The Order’s Arsenals: -10% artillery cost, +5% siege ability (20 years).

**Historical root:** Fortresses, stores, stables and workshops.

**Fictional continuation:** Fortification expertise and fiscal capacity support a temporary siege-logistics improvement.

## C07 — A World of Chapters

Slot 5, row 30. Required missions: C03, C04.

**Conditions:** Remain Catholic. Have 150 cities and 90% religious unity; complete any 2 of The Northern Rule, An Oceanic Order, A Southern Chapter and Arabian Concord.

**Rewards:** A World of Chapters: -5% state maintenance (permanent). +100 government reform progress.

**Historical root:** Commanderies and central administration.

**Fictional continuation:** Converge only two chosen theatres. The player does not have to conquer every frontier to build a coherent Order.

[Historical source](https://deutscher-orden.com/geschichte/)

## C08 — The Last General Chapter

Slot 5, row 36. Required missions: C07, C06.

**Conditions:** Remain Catholic. Be at peace with +3 stability, 95% religious unity, below 10% overextension and no loans. Finish any 2 of A Northern See, A Second Centre, The Eastern Marshal and Hospitaller Charter.

**Rewards:** Event: The Last General Chapter. Choose one outcome; rewards do not stack.

**Historical root:** Commanderies and central administration.

**Fictional continuation:** The final administrative ending remains available through several campaign combinations.
